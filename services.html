01
Diagnostics

Measure first.
Then treat.

Imaging comes before any treatment is recommended, for everyone. The other two are optional, carry their own cost, and are suggested only where the result would change what the plan says.

Visia Skin Analysis
Clinical Imaging

Eight features of the skin photographed under standardized light, including ultraviolet imaging that shows pigment damage sitting below the surface before it emerges. Separating the red channel from the brown is what distinguishes pigment from inflammation in deeper skin, where the two look identical to the eye.

Included in every consultation. Repeated at around six months to measure change.

Visia skin analysis →
Skin Microbiome Testing
Sequencing · Parallel Health

A swab that reads which organisms live on your skin, in what proportion, and what they are producing. It is a measurement of the present rather than a diagnosis, and its value shows up on the repeat test, after something has been changed deliberately. It is not a genetic test and reads none of your own DNA.

Optional, for an additional fee. Recommended where straightforward treatment has already failed.

Skin microbiome testing →
Lab Testing
Nutrient · Inflammatory · Hormonal

Individual markers chosen from the clinical picture rather than a fixed panel, where something systemic appears to be driving a skin problem. Never a general wellness screen. Any value outside the normal range is communicated to your primary care physician.

Optional, for an additional fee. Not ordered routinely. Recent results from your own physician are often enough.

Injectables

All injectable treatments are performed directly by Dr. Shankar. Placement, dosing, and sequencing are calibrated to your anatomy, your skin tone, and your goals.

Neuromodulators
Botox · Dysport

Neuromodulators relax targeted muscles to smooth dynamic wrinkles, lift specific areas, and subtly contour the face. Used for the forehead, glabella, crow's feet, brow lifting, lip lines, neck bands, and more.

Results visible in 3 to 14 days. Duration typically 3 to 4 months.

Dermal Fillers
Hyaluronic Acid

Hyaluronic acid fillers restore volume and define contours. Dr. Shankar approaches filler as a structural tool, with each placement considering facial balance, tissue depth, and long-term outcomes. Used for cheeks, lips, jawline, temples, and nasolabial folds.

Results immediate, refined over 2 weeks. Duration 9 to 18 months depending on area.

Dermal fillers →
Biostimulators
Sculptra · Radiesse

Rather than adding immediate volume, biostimulators signal your skin to produce its own collagen, creating gradual, lasting improvement in skin quality and structural support. Sculptra uses poly-L-lactic acid; Radiesse uses calcium hydroxylapatite. Both work best as part of a longer-term rejuvenation strategy.

Results develop over 4 to 12 weeks. Duration 1 to 2 years or more.

Sculptra and Radiesse →
SkinVive
Skin quality

A hyaluronic acid gel placed in the skin itself as small microdroplets rather than underneath it. It treats smoothness and hydration rather than shape, and it is FDA approved for the cheeks and, since June 2026, for lines on the neck.

Visible in 1 to 2 weeks, assessed at 1 month. Retreatment studied at around 6 months.

SkinVive →
PRP
Platelet-Rich Plasma

PRP concentrates your own growth factors from a small blood draw to stimulate tissue renewal. What it does depends on where it goes, so each use has its own page, its own protocol and its own account of the evidence behind it.

Series of treatments typically recommended. Hair restoration protocols available.

Lasers & devices

Technology chosen
for your skin.

Every device at Aahana is selected for its precision, its safety record across all skin tones, and the strength of its clinical evidence. Microneedling sits here too: it is a device treatment, performed by Dr. Mundluru.

XERF by Lutronic
Structural Skin Tightening · FDA Cleared

XERF delivers radiofrequency energy at two frequencies simultaneously, reaching every layer of the skin including the SMAS, the deep structural tissue responsible for lifting and firmness. Real-time impedance feedback adapts energy delivery to your skin continuously, ensuring precision and safety across all skin tones. Results build progressively as collagen remodels over weeks.

Single sessions and series available. Results continue developing over 3 to 6 months.

XERF skin tightening →
Aerolase Neo Elite
1064 nm Nd:YAG · FDA Cleared · All Skin Tones

A 1064 nm Nd:YAG laser that delivers energy in 650-microsecond pulses with no contact, no cooling, and no numbing. It is cleared for more than thirty indications, and at Aahana it is used primarily for acne, rosacea and persistent redness, melasma and post-inflammatory pigmentation, vascular lesions, scarring, and overall skin quality.

Typically a series, spaced two to four weeks apart. No numbing, no cooling, and no social downtime.

Botox for Chronic Migraine
Preventive

A series of small injections across areas of the head, neck and shoulders, aimed at how many headache days you have in a month rather than at any single headache. Prevention builds over two to four weeks, and some people need two treatment cycles for the full benefit.

Repeated every twelve weeks. A headache diary guides the dosing.

Occipital Nerve Block
Nerve Block

An injection near the nerves at the back of the head, for occipital neuralgia and for some migraines and cluster headaches. Relief can begin within minutes and can last weeks.

Repeated based on how long relief lasts, rather than on a fixed interval.
