Privacy policy

How information collected through this website is handled. Medical records are covered separately by our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Last updated 7 September 2026

This policy covers aahanasf.com. It does not cover the medical records of patients, which are governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices and by California law.

What this site collects

This website has no analytics, no advertising pixels and no tracking scripts. We do not know who you are when you read a page here.

Information reaches us in only three ways.

  • The waitlist form. If you join the waitlist, you give us the information you type into that form. It is delivered to us through Tally, the form service that hosts it.
  • Email. If you email us, we have your email address and whatever you write.
  • Server logs. Our host records standard request data, including IP address, browser type and the page requested. This is ordinary web server operation and is used for security and reliability.

Please do not send medical details, photographs of a condition, or anything else you would consider private through the waitlist form or by email. Neither is a secure channel. Those conversations belong in a consultation.

What it is used for

  • To contact you about opening, appointments and the waitlist.
  • To answer what you asked.
  • To keep the site running and secure.

We do not sell information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

Who else touches it

A handful of services are involved in delivering this website, and each one necessarily sees something.

  • Vercel hosts the site and records server logs.
  • Tally hosts the waitlist form and stores what you submit to it.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces. Loading a font transmits your IP address to Google.
  • cdnjs and jsDelivr serve two animation libraries, which likewise means your IP address reaches those networks.

Each of those has its own privacy policy, and their handling of your data is governed by it rather than by this page.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies of its own. The third parties listed above may set their own when their content loads, and the waitlist form may set a cookie while you are completing it.

How long we keep it

Waitlist entries are kept until the practice opens and for a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted. Email is kept as long as it is useful for the conversation it belongs to. Server logs are kept for a short period by our host as part of normal operation.

Your rights in California

If you live in California, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request that it be deleted, to request that it be corrected, and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of those rights.

To make a request, email hello@aahanaskin.com. We will need enough information to confirm who you are before acting on a request, and we will not use what you send for that purpose for anything else.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, email us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, which is the reason for the request above not to send anything medical through this website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Material changes will be described rather than quietly folded in.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to hello@aahanaskin.com.
