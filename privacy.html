Privacy policy

How information collected through this website is handled. Medical records are covered separately by our Notice of Privacy Practices.

Last updated 25 November 2026

This policy covers aahanasf.com. It does not cover the medical records of patients, which are governed by our Notice of Privacy Practices and by California law.

What this site collects

  • Visits measured by Patientfy, our host
  • We hear from you only via the waitlist, email or server logs
  • Don't send medical details here

This site is hosted by Patientfy, which measures visits so we can see which pages people find useful and how the site performs in search. Those tools may set cookies and record information such as the pages viewed, the device and browser used, and an approximate location.

Information reaches us in only three ways.

  • The waitlist form. If you join the waitlist, you give us the information you type into that form. It is delivered to us through Tally, the form service that hosts it. The mobile number field on that form is optional, and entering one is how you consent to be texted. See Text messages below.
  • Email. If you email us, we have your email address and whatever you write.
  • Server logs. Our host records standard request data, including IP address, browser type and the page requested. This is ordinary web server operation and is used for security and reliability.

Please do not send medical details, photographs of a condition, or anything else you would consider private through the waitlist form or by email. Neither is a secure channel. Those conversations belong in a consultation.

What it is used for

  • To contact you about opening, appointments and the waitlist.
  • To answer what you asked.
  • To keep the site running and secure.

We do not sell information. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it to build a profile of you.

Text messages

  • Optional, never required
  • Never sold or shared for marketing
  • Reply STOP to stop
  • Not for medical details

If you give us a mobile number and agree to be texted, we use it to send appointment reminders and scheduling messages, care instructions related to a treatment, and occasional practice updates. Agreeing to receive text messages is optional. It is not a condition of joining the waitlist, of booking, or of receiving care, and you can decline and still be contacted by email.

We will not share mobile information or personally identifiable information with third parties for marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile numbers are never sold, rented, or passed to advertisers or data brokers. They reach only the messaging and scheduling providers that send our texts on our behalf, and those providers are not permitted to use them for anything of their own.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply HELP to any message for help, and reply STOP or UNSUBSCRIBE to stop receiving texts. You can also ask us to remove your number at any time by emailing hello@aahanaskin.com. Mobile carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Text messaging is not a secure channel and is not the place for medical detail. Please do not send symptoms, photographs of a condition, or anything else you would consider private by text.

AI assistant

  • You'll always know it's automated
  • Books and answers questions, never gives medical advice
  • Collects only name, date of birth, phone or email
  • Ask for a person anytime

We use an automated assistant to help with scheduling and to answer questions about what we offer. It may answer our phone or appear on this website as a chat window. You will always be told that you are speaking with an automated assistant, and you can ask for a person at any point.

What it does. It books, moves and cancels appointments, and answers questions about our hours, location, services and pricing.

What it does not do. It does not give medical advice, assess symptoms, recommend treatment, or discuss medication. Those questions are for Dr. Mundluru, and the assistant will offer to book you in or have someone call you back.

What it collects. Only what is needed to book an appointment: your name, your date of birth, and your phone number or email address. It does not ask for medical information, and we ask that you do not give it any. If you mention something medical, the assistant does not add it to your record.

Who sees it. Your booking details go to Patientfy, which operates the assistant for us, and to Boulevard, which holds our schedule and our records. Both are bound by agreements requiring them to protect your information and to use it only to provide these services to us. We do not sell your information and we do not share it for marketing.

Improving the assistant. The assistant answers from information published on this website about our services, our hours, our location and our pricing. We also use the questions people ask about those things to improve its answers over time. This is about service information only. We do not use your name, date of birth or contact details for it, and no medical information is involved, because the assistant does not discuss medical subjects. We do not sell your information.

Your choices. You can decline to use the assistant entirely. Call or text 415-691-7603, or email hello@aahanaskin.com, and a person will help you. You can also ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it.

Who else touches it

  • Tally: the waitlist form
  • Patientfy: hosting, visit measurement and the scheduling assistant
  • Boulevard: booking, XERF page only

A handful of services are involved in delivering this website, and each one necessarily sees something.

  • Tally hosts the waitlist form and stores what you submit to it. Tally's code is not loaded while you are reading. It is fetched only at the moment you click to open the waitlist, so Tally is not told which pages you looked at.
  • Patientfy hosts this site. It records server logs and visit data so we can see how the site performs in search, and it operates the scheduling assistant described in the section above, receiving the booking details you give it.
  • Boulevard is our booking system. Its code loads on the XERF page and nowhere else on this site, where it runs the booking window and receives what you enter when you book. On every other page it is absent.

Each of those has its own privacy policy, and their handling of your data is governed by it rather than by this page.

Cookies

This site and the services listed above, including Patientfy's visit measurement, may set cookies. The waitlist form may also set a cookie while you are completing it. You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings; the site still works without them.

How long we keep it

Waitlist entries are kept until the practice opens and for a reasonable period afterwards, then deleted. Email is kept as long as it is useful for the conversation it belongs to. Server logs are kept for a short period by our host as part of normal operation.

Your rights in California

If you live in California, you may have the right to know what personal information we hold about you, to request that it be deleted, to request that it be corrected, and to be free from discrimination for exercising any of those rights.

To make a request, email hello@aahanaskin.com, or text or call 415-691-7603. We will need enough information to confirm who you are before acting on a request, and we will not use what you send for that purpose for anything else.

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under California law, so there is nothing to opt out of.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has submitted information to us, email us and we will delete it.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, which is the reason for the request above not to send anything medical through this website.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date at the top. Material changes will be described rather than quietly folded in.

Contact

Questions about this policy go to hello@aahanaskin.com. You can also text or call 415-691-7603.
