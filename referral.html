How referrals work
  • You get $100 off your first treatment or package of $300 or more. Just mention who referred you when you book.
  • Your friend gets $100 in Aahana credit once your treatment is paid.
  • Refer friends yourself and earn $100 in credit for each one who books and pays for a treatment of $300 or more. Credit adds up, so five referrals is $500.

Credit can be used on any Aahana service. It has no cash value and can't be transferred.
