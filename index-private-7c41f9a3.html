The rule

You never have to remember a page's status. Two things decide it, and both live in the files:

  • Public means listed in sitemap.xml with no robots tag.
  • Unlisted means noindex, nofollow, noarchive, absent from sitemap.xml, and absent from the nav.

The one way to break it: a link from a public page to an unlisted one. That is how a crawler finds things, and it would expose the whole care set at once. The audit at the top of this page checks for exactly that every time the page is rebuilt.
